Hi there! Welcome to the ZIP CTF, a Capture-the-Flag challenge with a file compression theme.

Your goal is to advance through a series of stages with dedicated webpages to reach the end of the scavenger hunt. You'll find the "flag" phrase on each page, which will then lead you to the next page in the hunt. Each page will be found at "https://giragon6.github.io/ctf/{flag}" (replace {flag} with the flag for the page). Ex: if the phrase is "stegosaurus", you'd go to: https://giragon.github.io/ctf/stegosaurus.
